Making an interactive banking system for myself to play with.

All Connected Payments Interface (ACPI):
    is a payment managing body for the initiated payment by the user.
    It initiated the tranfer or moving of the money from sender to receiver after the confirmation of the payment.
    It keeps a record of the money sent and received by the user.
    manages the banks to perform actions.


Workers:
    Server ( ACPI )
    Banks ( Accounter ) => These will contain the accounts and will be used to transfer money.
    Client ( PSP (Payment Service Provider) ) => initiates a payment.

functions: 
    Concept of Money tranfers, Sending and Recieving.
    Accounts: Saving 

How will it work on frontend.
    To start with the System, user will be prompted to download the Client Application.
    After the installs the application, he will be prompted for privacy policy agreement and other information.
    Then he will prompted to make a login or sign up request (mandatory). 
    After the Whole login process, user will be able to send or recieve Money.

    User will be after to scan the qr code displayed on the bank to request money upto some limit.
    The bank after verification will send the money from the bank's account to user's account.
